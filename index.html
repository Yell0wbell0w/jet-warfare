How To Play

  • Drag your jet left/right along the bottom with your mouse or finger. If a keyboard is connected, use the Left/Right arrow keys or A/D to move instead.
  • Your jet fires automatically — barrels, burst count and fire speed depend on your jet.
  • Every level has 5 waves plus a boss that holds position at the top.
  • If an enemy touches your jet you die instantly. If one reaches the bottom without being destroyed, you instantly lose.
  • Enemies shoot back — losing all your health ends the run. A shot that flies past you without hitting simply fizzles out.
