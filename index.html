How To Play

  • Drag your jet left/right along the bottom with your mouse or finger. If a keyboard is connected, use the Left/Right arrow keys or A/D to move instead.
  • Your jet fires automatically — barrels, burst count and fire speed depend on your jet.
  • Every level has 5 waves plus a boss that holds position at the top.
  • If an enemy touches your jet you die instantly. If one reaches the bottom without being destroyed, you instantly lose.
  • Enemies shoot back — losing all your health ends the run. A shot that flies past you without hitting simply fizzles out.
  • Green drops near the bottom heal 25% of your max health when you drag over them. Yellow drops temporarily boost fire rate, and red drops temporarily boost damage.
  • Skills come in three flavors: Manual skills only fire when you tap the purple SKILL button (or press Q on a keyboard); Passive skills work by themselves all the time; Auto skills (like Energy Shield) recharge and trigger on their own on a timer. The skill page tells you which type your jet has.
  • A jet's skill level rises by 1 every 2 levels you clear while flying it, up to Lv.5. A newly obtained jet always starts its skill at Lv.1.
  • Clearing a level for the very first time pays out double coins and gems versus a replay.
  • Earn Coins and Gems to unlock jets on the Jets page. Preview any jet to see its skill in action — including a dedicated FIRE preview — then spend coins to upgrade the skill, or just win more fights to level it up naturally, up to Lv.5. You can refund a coin-bought skill upgrade for its coins back.
  • Replay any beaten level anytime from Levels.
